Why a specialist

A children's dentist is not just a dentist who sees children

Pediatric dentistry is its own specialty. It covers how children's teeth grow, how habits shape a jaw, how to treat a nervous four-year-old without frightening them, and how to spot the problems that only show up in a growing mouth.

My training is in exactly that: a Bachelor of Dental Surgery, a Master's degree, a pediatric dentistry certificate, American Board certification and a Canadian Fellowship from the Royal College of Dentists of Canada.

A particular focus

Dentistry for children with special needs

Alongside pediatric dentistry, I am a consultant in dentistry for people with special needs. Many families are told their child is "too difficult" to treat. Usually what's needed is more time, a different approach, and someone trained for it.

Pace

Time, not rushing

Appointments paced to your child rather than to a schedule — including short familiarisation visits where nothing clinical happens at all.
