# Version 3 — "Conversion-led"

**Methodology:** A — Raw creation, structurally modelled on the research winner

**Named direction:** Bright, dense, high-contrast and utilitarian. The booking form sits *above
the fold* inside the hero. Steel blue and a green action colour on white, small radii, tight
spacing. Emotionally it sells **"book right now, it's easy."**

## Why this structure

Dima Dental Center scored highest in the research (79/100) not on reviews but on structure:
cited authority, a genuine comparison table, an explicit special-needs protocol, an emergency
guide, and a structured booking form alongside WhatsApp. This version reproduces that
*architecture* in Dr. Alaa's brand and voice.

| Element | Source pattern |
|---|---|
| Booking form in the hero | Report principle 4 — largest controllable conversion gap |
| Credential stats strip | Report playbook 2 — proof adjacent to every CTA |
| Comparison table | Dima Dental's general-vs-specialist table |
| FAQ accordion | Parent-education pattern, playbook 1 |
| Emergency band | Dima's tooth-emergency guide |
| Sticky mobile CTA bar | Beats DM-only contact on the device parents actually use |

## Design decisions

| Dimension | Choice |
|---|---|
| Layout | Two-column hero with a floating form card; dense modular bands |
| Type | System sans, heavy weights, tight tracking, smaller sizes than V1/V2 |
| Colour | White/cool-grey base, steel blue structural, green as the single action colour, red emergency band |
| Shape | Small radii (8–14px), bordered cards rather than shadows |
| Density | High — the most content of the three versions |
| Motion | Micro only: hover lift, accordion toggle |

## How it diverges from V1 and V2

V1 is warm coral on sand and airy; V2 is navy serif editorial with hairline rules; V3 is bright
white with a green action colour, a data table, an accordion and a sticky bar. It is the only
version with a form above the fold, and the only one using green or red at all.

## Report principles honoured

Same substance and copy as V1 and V2. All ten principles honoured, with principle 4 (structured
booking) and principle 7 (parent education) implemented most aggressively here.

## Deviations & flags

- **No "free first consultation."** Dima offers one and it clearly converts, but there is no
  evidence Dr. Alaa does — inventing an offer was not an option. Worth asking her about.
- **No cited statistics.** Dima cites a Saudi Ministry of Health cavity statistic. It was not
  verified from the primary source during research, so it is deliberately not reproduced here.
- **Comparison table and FAQ answers** describe the specialty and a standard specialist
  approach in general terms, flagged `CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH` — Dr. Alaa should review the
  wording so it matches how she actually works.
- `treatment-room.png` dropped (stock-style surgical image), no reviews, no pricing, no opening
  hours — as in V1 and V2.
